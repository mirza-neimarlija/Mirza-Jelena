The Idea

Not just a wedding day — a few days together by the Mediterranean, from the moment you land to the last dance on the sand.

We wanted our closest people around us for longer than one evening allows, so we've built the whole weekend around it — arrival, a welcome dinner, the ceremony, and a slow morning after to say goodbye properly.

Belek, Antalya

Where the Mediterranean meets the coast.

A stretch of pine forest and pale sand along Turkey's Turquoise Coast, an hour's drive south-east of Antalya. Warm water well into October, quiet beaches, and the kind of light that turns gold an hour before sunset — the reason we chose it.

The Ceremony

An elegant beach setup with minimal florals and natural textures, the sea itself as the backdrop.

The Tablescape

Ivory linen, sophisticated glassware and candlelight, with restrained turquoise accents.

The Flowers

Ivory, cream and soft green — natural foliage over anything that reads as a generic bouquet.

Texture

Sea glass, driftwood and stone — the tactile materials behind every surface.

Shells

Small natural details inspired by the shoreline, used sparingly near headings and place cards.

Golden Hour

The moment the palette comes alive — daylight softening into candlelight by 19:30.

The Beach

Sand, sea and shell become part of the design itself, not a backdrop to it.

The Evening

Candles, warm ambient light and the sea at night — a quieter, more intimate register.

A Note On The Design

Nothing loud. Just the sea, warm light, and a few small details done properly.

Every detail — from the aisle to the last candle — is drawn from the same palette: Mediterranean turquoise, warm sand and ivory, with a little gold at the edges. If you'd like to see the resort itself before the day, the gallery below has more.
